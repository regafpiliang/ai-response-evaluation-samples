# Sample 03 — Indonesian Language Quality Evaluation

## Task Type
Indonesian Language AI Evaluation

## Evaluation Focus
- Natural Indonesian language
- Tone
- Cultural appropriateness
- Clarity
- Contextual language use

## User Prompt

> Buatkan pesan WhatsApp yang sopan untuk menanyakan kembali hasil interview kerja kepada HR setelah beberapa hari belum mendapat kabar.

## Response A

> Halo HR. Saya ingin mengejar kembali mengenai hasil interview saya beberapa hari lalu. Tolong memberikan informasi terbaru secepatnya. Terima kasih.

## Response B

> Halo Kak, selamat siang. Saya ingin melakukan follow-up terkait proses interview yang saya jalani beberapa hari lalu. Apabila sudah ada informasi mengenai tahap selanjutnya, saya sangat berterima kasih jika dapat diinformasikan. Terima kasih atas waktunya.

## Evaluation

### Response A

**Language Naturalness:** Moderate  
The phrase "mengejar kembali" sounds unnatural in Indonesian professional communication and appears to be a literal translation of the English phrase "follow up."

**Tone:** Weak to Moderate  
The phrase "secepatnya" may sound demanding depending on the relationship with the recruiter.

**Clarity:** Good  
The purpose of the message is understandable.

**Cultural Appropriateness:** Moderate  
The message could be more polite and context-sensitive for Indonesian professional communication.

### Response B

**Language Naturalness:** Strong  
The wording sounds natural and commonly used in Indonesian professional communication.

**Tone:** Strong  
The message is respectful without sounding excessively formal.

**Clarity:** Strong  
The purpose is clear.

**Cultural Appropriateness:** Strong  
Using a polite greeting and indirect request is appropriate for many Indonesian workplace contexts.

## Preferred Response

**Response B**

## Reasoning

Response B is more natural and culturally appropriate for Indonesian professional communication. It communicates the request clearly while maintaining a polite tone.

Response A demonstrates how literal translation from English can create phrases that are grammatically understandable but unnatural in everyday Indonesian.

## Suggested Improvement

A shorter version of Response B could also be used when the recruiter and applicant already have an established informal communication style.

Example:

> Halo Kak, izin follow-up terkait interview saya beberapa hari lalu. Apakah sudah ada update mengenai proses selanjutnya? Terima kasih sebelumnya.
