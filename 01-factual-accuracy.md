# Sample 01 — Factual Accuracy Evaluation

## Task Type
AI Response Evaluation

## Evaluation Focus
- Factual accuracy
- Completeness
- Clarity
- Relevance

## User Prompt

> What is the capital of Australia, and why do some people incorrectly think Sydney is the capital?

## Response A

> The capital of Australia is Sydney. It is the country's largest and most internationally recognized city, so it became the national capital.

## Response B

> The capital of Australia is Canberra. Sydney is often mistaken for the capital because it is Australia's largest and most internationally recognized city. Canberra was selected as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne.

## Evaluation

### Response A

**Accuracy:** Poor  
The response contains a major factual error because Sydney is not the capital of Australia.

**Relevance:** Good  
The answer attempts to respond directly to the question.

**Clarity:** Good  
The wording is simple and easy to understand.

**Overall assessment:**  
The response is clear but factually incorrect, which makes it unreliable.

### Response B

**Accuracy:** Strong  
The response correctly identifies Canberra as the capital of Australia and provides relevant historical context.

**Relevance:** Strong  
It directly answers both parts of the question.

**Clarity:** Strong  
The explanation is concise and understandable.

**Overall assessment:**  
The response is accurate, relevant, and sufficiently complete.

## Preferred Response

**Response B**

## Reasoning

Response B should be preferred because factual correctness is essential. It answers the main question correctly and also explains the common misconception about Sydney.

## Suggested Improvement

Response B could be improved by adding a little more context, such as the fact that Canberra was officially selected in the early 20th century as a compromise location between Sydney and Melbourne.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Fact checking
- Error identification
- Comparative response evaluation
- Clear written reasoning
- Quality assessment
